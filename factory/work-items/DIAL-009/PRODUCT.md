# DIAL-009 — broader radio discovery and NTS

## Problem and decision for approval

Dial's finite stream policy yields sparse choices for several countries. The owner wants more useful stations in the countries already offered, more countries, NTS 1 and 2, and a clean, minimal ChatGPT desktop player. Approve this bounded candidate set and a separate NTS collection. A globe picker is a separate stretch proposal, outside this build.

This spec proposes **one newly researched candidate in each existing country** (US, FR, JP, GB, CA, DE, ES, IT) and **one candidate in each of eight added countries** (AU, BR, MX, KR, IN, ZA, NL, SE). The exact 16 UUID and stream URL pairs are in TECH.md. The selection is a reviewable first increment, not a claim of complete country coverage. Radio Browser rows, direct stream responses, browser CSP acceptance, and audible playback are different evidence levels.

## Behavior and desktop UX

- The player keeps one compact, neutral card: station name, short metadata, clear Play/Pause, playback/error status, and existing same-browser remote pause. Beneath it, a two-choice navigation control offers **Countries** and **NTS**. Countries shows a compact labeled country picker with the 16 researched countries, the active country, the existing result summary and station list. The country tool remains usable for other assigned ISO codes; the picker makes the researched set discoverable. NTS shows two clearly named rows, **NTS 1** and **NTS 2**, attributed to NTS, with a link to [NTS live radio](https://www.nts.live/radio). NTS has no invented UK or other country label. [NTS describes](https://www.nts.live/about) two 24/7 channels and broadcasters in over 80 countries; [its support page](https://ntslive.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/77000569502-live-radio/) describes London and LA studios and international shows.
- Selecting a country row or an NTS row prepares it; Play is still a separate explicit action. Switching collections or changing a country while audio plays does not start a second stream. The existing player coordination controls any eligible stream. A new country request retains the DIAL-008 reset behavior; More or Retry does not change selection or audio. Playback errors identify failure and leave a usable choice list. Do not label a catalog row “playable” until actual host playback is observed.
- For a country, keep the DIAL-008 three windows at offsets 0/50/100, at most 50 provider rows per window, 30 unique compatible choices, initial reveal of eight, More only for already known hidden choices, and manual Retry for partial scans. Preserve its truthful counts and cap/partial wording, signed click reporting, Radio Browser attribution and IP disclosure, safe text rendering, and no automatic audio. Added candidates must not expand the scan budget.
- The NTS collection is present with both official channel names and source link. In-player Play for NTS is enabled **only if the NTS compatibility gate in TECH.md passes** and receives separate independent Review and Verify evidence. If that gate is unresolved or fails, NTS rows offer **Open on NTS** and clearly say in-player listening is unavailable. They must not silently use a broad wildcard, transient edge URL, or an unreviewed proxy. NTS is not a Radio Browser row, so its play must not call Radio Browser click reporting or show the country metadata/disclosure as if it did.
- Keep a simple visual hierarchy, restrained spacing and color, legible long station names, visible focus, 44 px or larger touch/click targets, and responsive narrow/light/dark layouts. Avoid a visual globe, maps, autoplay, artwork feeds, animations, or cluttered station badges. The current compact player and disclosure remain recognizable.

## Dated research and limits

On **2026-11-12 03:38–03:41 UTC**, the Spec worker queried Radio Browser's exact-country endpoint at `https://de1.api.radio-browser.info` with `order=votes&reverse=true&limit=50&hidebroken=true`. For the eight existing countries, it sampled offsets 0, 50, and 100; for added countries, offset 0. Every sampled request succeeded and returned 50 rows. The candidate pairs in TECH.md were seen in those samples. This establishes directory presence only at that time. A request made later can reorder or remove them; first-page samples do not establish total coverage. Prior [DIAL-008 scope](../DIAL-008/PRODUCT.md) recorded 2026-11-08 compatible catalog counts US 9, FR 6, JP 1, GB 6, CA 2, DE 1, ES 1, IT 5 under the then-current finite origins. Those are historical counts, not current guarantees.

Each proposed exact URL received one no-redirect, 8-second, 256-byte capped request on 2026-11-12. All 16 candidates returned HTTP 200 (US 206), `audio/mpeg`, and initial MP3 frame bytes; the full result and excluded leads are in TECH.md. These checks do not establish audible ChatGPT playback or CSP permission. [Official NTS support](https://ntslive.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/77000587257-tunein) lists the two direct URLs. Today's requests first returned 302 to `streams.radiomast.io`, then 302 to an `audio-edge-…radiomast.io` host, then 200 `audio/mpeg` and MP3 bytes. The edge address can change; browser CSP acceptance remains unknown.

## Non-goals and edge cases

No claim of all stations in a country; no arbitrary station URL input, broad origin trust, new provider, codec expansion, playlist format, accounts, favorites, recommendations, persistence, streaming proxy, hosting, deployment, merge, or publication. A country with no compatible choices says so for the bounded windows only. Duplicate UUIDs or canonical URLs appear once. Provider failures stay partial and retryable. If a researched row disappears, redirects, changes country/codec/URL, or fails a fresh stream check, it is excluded and reported as a coverage gap; no quiet substitution. If NTS's destination changes, its in-player gate returns to blocked while the official link remains.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The 16-country picker and country tool expose the researched countries, with exact-country MP3 results only. The 16 candidate UUID/URL pairs are admitted only while their current provider rows still match the fixed policy. For each country, fresh evidence records directory presence, exclusion, count, and any drift; missing pairs are explicit gaps.
2. A fresh independent direct probe for each admitted pair records status, content type, redirect behavior and capped MP3 bytes. A direct probe is not called ChatGPT playback. Changes to any URL/origin require new spec approval, not an automatic substitution.
3. Country scans preserve the DIAL-008 limits, 8-item local reveal, known-choice More, partial/manual Retry, stale-result guards, safe text, selection/audio continuity, exact CSP, signed click reporting and its disclosure.
4. NTS 1 and NTS 2 are plainly discoverable as NTS editorial channels with the official link and no country claim. In-player controls are enabled only after the narrow compatibility gate passes. Otherwise both official links and an honest unavailable state work. NTS plays never report a Radio Browser click.
5. The player is compact, keyboard accessible, usable at narrow and desktop widths in light/dark host themes, and has clear Play/Pause and failure states. Independent Review and Verify bind to the exact candidate commit. Verify records catalog admission, direct reachability, CSP, and actual ChatGPT audio separately; absent host playback or layout evidence is BLOCKED, never inferred from unit tests.
